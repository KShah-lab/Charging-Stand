# Vertical Galaxy Watch Charging Stand — Project Journal

## Overview
#### This journal documents the design and development of a custom vertical charging stand for the Samsung Galaxy Watch. The aim was to create a portable, minimal, and filament‑efficient stand built around the existing charging puck.

## Initial Goals
#### - Reduce visible cable length for a cleaner look  
#### - Make the stand portable  
#### - Keep the design visually appealing  
#### - Allow the charging puck to slide in and out  
#### - Ensure the stand is strong enough to hold the watch  
#### - Keep the model simple and easy to print  
#### - Minimise filament usage  

## Initial Research
#### I explored existing designs on MakerWorld, mainly stands built for the iPhone MagSafe puck. While these designs looked promising, they were designed to support the weight of an iPhone, meaning unnecessary bulk and excess filament. This went against one of the main goals of the project — efficiency.

#### Because of this, I decided to design a fully custom model specifically for the Galaxy Watch.

## First Design Direction
### The first custom concept included:
#### - A channel for the cable to loop through  
#### - A slot for the puck to rest securely  
#### - A stopper to prevent the watch from slipping  
#### - The ability for the stand to double as a phone stand  
#### - Reduced thickness to keep print time low while still fitting the puck  

## Early Challenges
#### I struggled heavily with CAD at the start. I created dozens of sketches and prototypes, but most were:
#### - Impractical  
#### - Too bulky  
#### - Not printable  
#### - Or just looked plain stupid  

#### The first design attempt had space for the cable to run underneath the stand, but the overall structure still didn’t meet the goals of portability and minimal filament usage. More iterations were needed to refine the concept.
